Cherry Systems

A friendly Linux computer that runs your Windows apps, an app store anyone can publish to, and an easy way to bring your files along.

Cherry OS

A desktop Linux based on Debian. Windows apps just work, five desktops to choose from, programming languages built in, and security updates until 2030.

Cherry Store

Apps published by anyone, with no review queue. Every app is signed and virus-scanned. Built into Cherry OS, and a free download for Ubuntu, Linux Mint, Debian and more.

Cherry Move

Moves your files from your old Windows, Mac or Linux computer by USB stick, cloud drive or home network, encrypted the whole way.

An open app store

Every Cherry Store app, the checks it went through and the app list itself are public in Cherry-Systems/cherry-store on GitHub. Anyone can publish, and anyone can report a harmful app.
